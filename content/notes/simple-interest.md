## Formula table (plain text)

| Quantity | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| Simple interest | I = P x r x t |
| Amount | A = P + I |
| Rate as decimal | 4% = 0.04 |

P = principal (starting money), r = yearly rate as a decimal, t = years.

## Why

Interest for 1 year is P x r.  
For t years on the same principal (no compounding): I = P x r x t.

## Number examples

| P | r | t | Interest I | Amount A |
|---|---|---|------------|----------|
| 2000 | 0.04 | 3 | 2000 x 0.04 x 3 = 240 | 2240 |
| 5000 | 0.03 | 2 | 5000 x 0.03 x 2 = 300 | 5300 |
| 800 | 0.05 | 4 | 800 x 0.05 x 4 = 160 | 960 |

## Remember

Simple interest does **not** add interest onto the principal each year.
