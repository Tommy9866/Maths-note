Simple interest is calculated on the **original principal** only.

$$I = Prt$$

where:

- $P$ = principal
- $r$ = interest rate per year (as a decimal)
- $t$ = time in years
- $I$ = interest earned

Amount after $t$ years:

$$A = P + I = P(1 + rt)$$

Example: $\$2000$ at $4\%$ p.a. for $3$ years:

$$I = 2000 \times 0.04 \times 3 = \$240$$
