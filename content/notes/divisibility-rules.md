## Quick check list

| Divisible by | What to check |
|--------------|---------------|
| 2 | Last digit even? (0, 2, 4, 6, 8) |
| 3 | Digit sum divisible by 3? |
| 4 | Last 2 digits divisible by 4? |
| 5 | Last digit 0 or 5? |
| 6 | Passes rule for 2 **and** rule for 3? |
| 9 | Digit sum divisible by 9? |
| 10 | Last digit 0? |

## Remember

- For **2, 5, 10**: look only at the **last digit**.
- For **4**: look only at the **last 2 digits**.
- For **3 and 9**: add the digits.
- For **6**: you need **both** the 2-rule and the 3-rule.
