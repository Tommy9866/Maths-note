An integer is **divisible** by another if the remainder is $0$.

## Common tests

| Divisible by | Rule |
|--------------|------|
| $2$ | Units digit is even ($0,2,4,6,8$) |
| $3$ | Sum of digits is divisible by $3$ |
| $4$ | Last **two** digits form a number divisible by $4$ |
| $5$ | Units digit is $0$ or $5$ |
| $6$ | Divisible by **both** $2$ and $3$ |
| $8$ | Last **three** digits form a number divisible by $8$ |
| $9$ | Sum of digits is divisible by $9$ |
| $10$ | Units digit is $0$ |

## Examples

- $612$: last two digits $12$, and $12 \div 4 = 3$ → divisible by $4$
- $468$: digit sum $4+6+8=18$, divisible by $3$ and by $9$
- $288$: even, and digit sum $18$ divisible by $3$ → divisible by $6$
