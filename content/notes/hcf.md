The **H.C.F.** (also called **G.C.D.**) of two or more integers is the **largest** positive integer that divides all of them.

## Using prime factorization

1. Factorise each integer into primes (index form)
2. For each **common** prime, take the **smallest** index
3. Multiply those chosen primes

### Example

$$56 = 2^3 \times 7,\qquad 84 = 2^2 \times 3 \times 7$$

Common primes: $2$ (min index $2$) and $7$ (min index $1$)

$$\text{H.C.F.} = 2^2 \times 7 = 28$$

## Using short division

Divide the numbers together by common primes until no common prime remains; multiply those divisors to get the H.C.F.
