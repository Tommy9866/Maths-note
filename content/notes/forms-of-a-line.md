## Common forms

- Point-slope: $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$
- Slope-intercept: $y = mx + c$
- Two-point: $\dfrac{y - y_1}{x - x_1} = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$
- General: $ax + by + c = 0$
