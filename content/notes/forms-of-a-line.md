## Formula table

| Form | Equation |
|------|----------|
| Slope-intercept | $y=mx+c$ |
| Point-slope | $y-y_1=m(x-x_1)$ |
| Two-point | $\dfrac{y-y_1}{x-x_1}=\dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$ |
| General | $ax+by+c=0$ |

## Derivation (point-slope)

A point $(x,y)$ on the line through $(x_1,y_1)$ with slope $m$ satisfies

$$m=\frac{y-y_1}{x-x_1}\implies y-y_1=m(x-x_1)$$

## Number examples

| Given | Equation |
|-------|----------|
| slope $2$, through $(1,3)$ | $y-3=2(x-1)$ → $y=2x+1$ |
| points $(1,2)$, $(4,8)$ | $y-2=2(x-1)$ → $y=2x$ |
| $2x-y+3=0$ | $y=2x+3$ |
