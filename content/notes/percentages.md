A percentage is a fraction of $100$:

$$p\% = \frac{p}{100}$$

## Useful conversions

- Fraction → percentage: multiply by $100\%$
- Percentage change:

$$\text{Percentage change} = \frac{\text{new} - \text{original}}{\text{original}} \times 100\%$$
