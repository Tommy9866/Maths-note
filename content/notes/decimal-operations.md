## Addition and subtraction

Line up the decimal points, then add or subtract as usual.

$$82.5 + 7.64 = 90.14$$

$$53.1 - 2.48 = 50.62$$

## Multiplication

Ignore decimal points first, multiply, then place the decimal so that:

$$\text{decimal places in answer} = \text{sum of decimal places in the factors}$$

$$2.43 \times 1.5 = 3.645 \quad (2 + 1 = 3 \text{ d.p.})$$

## Division by a decimal

Move the decimal point in the **divisor** and **dividend** by the same number of places so the divisor becomes an integer. Place the decimal point in the quotient above the new decimal point in the dividend.

$$9.35 \div 2.5 = 3.74$$

## Mixed × and ÷

Work **from left to right**:

$$4.6 \times 0.5 \times 4 = 9.2 \times 4 = 36.8$$

Do **not** jump ahead to multiply $0.5 \times 4$ first unless brackets say so.
