For $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, the discriminant is:

$$\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$$

[[diagram:parabola|Shape of the quadratic helps interpret the roots]]

- $\Delta > 0$ → two distinct real roots
- $\Delta = 0$ → one repeated real root
- $\Delta < 0$ → no real roots (two complex conjugate roots)
