## Plane figures

- Rectangle: $A = lw$
- Triangle: $A = \frac{1}{2}bh$
- Circle: $A = \pi r^2$, $C = 2\pi r$

## Solids

- Prism: $V = \text{base area} \times \text{height}$
- Cylinder: $V = \pi r^2 h$
- Sphere: $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$
