**Prime factorization** means writing an integer as a product of prime factors.

[[diagram:factor-tree|Factor tree for 36]]

## Method

1. Start from the smallest prime factor $2$
2. Keep factorising until every factor is prime
3. Write the answer in **index form** if a prime repeats

## Examples

$$20 = 2 \times 10 = 2 \times 2 \times 5 = 2^2 \times 5$$

$$36 = 2 \times 18 = 2 \times 2 \times 9 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 = 2^2 \times 3^2$$

You can also use **short division** by primes instead of a factor tree.
