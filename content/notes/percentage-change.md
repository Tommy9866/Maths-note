Percentage change compares a new value with an original value:

$$\text{Percentage change} = \frac{\text{new} - \text{original}}{\text{original}} \times 100\%$$

[[diagram:percentage-bar|Increase or decrease as a share of the original]]

- Positive result → percentage **increase**
- Negative result → percentage **decrease**

Example: from $80$ to $100$:

$$\frac{100 - 80}{80} \times 100\% = 25\% \text{ increase}$$
