The **L.C.M.** of two or more integers is the **smallest** positive integer that is a multiple of all of them.

## Using prime factorization

1. Factorise each integer into primes (index form)
2. For **every** prime that appears, take the **largest** index
3. Multiply those chosen primes

### Example

$$18 = 2 \times 3^2,\qquad 60 = 2^2 \times 3 \times 5$$

Take $2^2$, $3^2$, and $5$:

$$\text{L.C.M.} = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5 = 180$$

## Using short division

Divide by common primes, then multiply **all** the divisors and remaining factors to get the L.C.M.
