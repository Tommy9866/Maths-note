## Formula table

| Ratio | Definition | Identity |
|-------|------------|----------|
| $\sin\theta$ | $\dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$ | — |
| $\cos\theta$ | $\dfrac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$ | — |
| $\tan\theta$ | $\dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$ | $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$ |
| Pythagoras link | — | $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$ |

## Derivation of $\tan\theta=\sin\theta/\cos\theta$

$$
\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}
=\frac{\text{opp}/\text{hyp}}{\text{adj}/\text{hyp}}
=\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}
=\tan\theta
$$

## Number examples

In a right triangle with opposite $=3$, adjacent $=4$, hypotenuse $=5$:

| Ratio | Value |
|-------|-------|
| $\sin\theta$ | $3/5=0.6$ |
| $\cos\theta$ | $4/5=0.8$ |
| $\tan\theta$ | $3/4=0.75$ |
| Check | $0.6^2+0.8^2=1$ |
