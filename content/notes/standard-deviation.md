## Formula table

| Measure | Population formula |
|---------|--------------------|
| Mean | $\mu=\dfrac{1}{n}\sum x_i$ |
| Variance | $\sigma^2=\dfrac{1}{n}\sum(x_i-\mu)^2$ |
| Standard deviation | $\sigma=\sqrt{\sigma^2}$ |

[[diagram:box-plot|Spread can also be seen from a box plot]]

## Derivation / meaning

$(x_i-\mu)^2$ measures squared distance from the mean.  
Averaging those squared distances gives variance; the square root returns to the original units as standard deviation.

## Number examples

Data: $2,4,4,6$

| Step | Value |
|------|-------|
| Mean $\mu$ | $(2+4+4+6)/4=4$ |
| Squared deviations | $4,0,0,4$ |
| Variance $\sigma^2$ | $(4+0+0+4)/4=2$ |
| Standard deviation $\sigma$ | $\sqrt{2}$ |
