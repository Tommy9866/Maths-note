## Formula table

| Figure | Area | Perimeter / Circumference |
|--------|------|---------------------------|
| Rectangle | $A=lw$ | $P=2(l+w)$ |
| Square | $A=s^2$ | $P=4s$ |
| Triangle | $A=\dfrac{1}{2}bh$ | sum of sides |
| Trapezium | $A=\dfrac{1}{2}(a+b)h$ | sum of sides |
| Circle | $A=\pi r^2$ | $C=2\pi r$ |

## Short derivation ideas

- Rectangle: cover with unit squares → length × width.  
- Triangle: two congruent triangles make a parallelogram / rectangle of area $bh$.  
- Circle: circumference $2\pi r$; area comes from rearranging sectors into an approximate rectangle of sides $r$ and $\pi r$.

## Number examples

| Figure | Data | Result |
|--------|------|--------|

| Trapezium | $a=6$, $b=10$, $h=4$ | $A=32$ |
