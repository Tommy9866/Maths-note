## Common formulas

| Figure | Area | Perimeter / Circumference |
|--------|------|---------------------------|
| Rectangle | $A = lw$ | $P = 2(l + w)$ |
| Triangle | $A = \frac{1}{2}bh$ | sum of sides |
| Circle | $A = \pi r^2$ | $C = 2\pi r$ |
| Trapezium | $A = \frac{1}{2}(a + b)h$ | sum of sides |
