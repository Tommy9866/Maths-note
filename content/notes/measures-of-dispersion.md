Common measures of spread for a data set:

- Range $= \text{max} - \text{min}$
- Interquartile range $= Q_3 - Q_1$
- Variance and standard deviation

For a population with mean $\mu$:

$$\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i - \mu)^2}$$
