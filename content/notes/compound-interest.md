Compound interest is calculated on the principal **plus** interest already earned.

$$A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}$$

where:

- $P$ = principal
- $r$ = annual interest rate (decimal)
- $n$ = number of compounding periods per year
- $t$ = time in years
- $A$ = final amount

Interest earned: $I = A - P$

If compounded yearly ($n = 1$):

$$A = P(1 + r)^t$$
